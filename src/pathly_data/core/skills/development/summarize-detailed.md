# summarize (detailed)

Produce a **detailed summary** of the source document: for each major section, its topic AND
its key points or decisions (names, APIs, identifiers, decisions, sequence). Optimizes for
recall (the artifact surfaces for queries about any of its sections).

## Output format — follow EXACTLY

Output these two sections and nothing else:

## Description

1–2 sentences of context: what this document is and why it matters. Plain prose, no list.

<summary_format>
