# summarize

Produce a compact **topic map** of the source document: the most important subjects only, each
with a short gloss describing what that section is about (not its details). Balanced
recall/precision.

## Output format — follow EXACTLY

Output these two sections and nothing else:

## Description

1–2 sentences of context: what this document is and why it matters. Plain prose, no list.

## Summary

<summary_format>
