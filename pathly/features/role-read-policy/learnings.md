trap, a quirk of the environment. Append one or two lines, newest last, tagged with your
ticket number. Never edit or delete another line. Keep it short; a human prunes it.

- (10) A real spawn is testable end to end with a fake broadcaster: `_run_stage_via_terminal(state, ..., run_id, broadcast)` where `broadcast` ignores every payload but `TERMINAL_SPAWN`, does the agent's work (Flask test client plus `X-Pathly-Run-Token`) while the token is live, then calls `get_run(run_id).mark_started()` and `.mark_pty_result({"result": {}, "exit_code": 0})`. To get the identity a handler would see, call the real `caller_identity(flask.request)` inside `app.test_request_context(headers={...})` before the spawn settles; afterwards the token is dead and the identity is `anonymous`.

- (10 r1) The raw run token is on three SSE channels, not one: `sse._broadcast_runner` sends the whole `TERMINAL_SPAWN` payload to the topic's `/events/runner` clients, every `/events/spawn` client and the `/events/runs` clients of its `run_id`, and `/events/*` skips the secret check, so any client that can subscribe can read a live token until the spawn settles. Only the two hosts' listeners read the payload's `run_token` field (both on `/events/spawn`): strip it from the other two channels, or hand it to the host another way, before any wall relies on `author_source="token"` (`/events/spawn` itself stays open to any client).
